# Security audit front paused after round 28 (cycle closed) until ADR005V01 and ADR007V01's deferred implementations land

**Reopen-when:** ADR005V01's FailureCodes/Constants registry AND ADR007V01's folderlog independence are both implemented, or before the next public release, whichever comes first

The 'Hardening de Superficies de Falsificacao e Exaustao de Memoria' cycle (rounds 17-28, see CYCLES.md) is now closed, ending the Security audit front's current run (rounds 22-28, 7 consecutive rounds, every one carrying at least one High-severity finding -- see the calibration table shown alongside this closure). Rather than starting another round immediately, this front is deliberately paused until two currently-Accepted-but-not-yet-implemented ADRs land: ADR005V01 (a central FailureCodes/Constants registry, replacing today's scattered failure-code literals and constants) and ADR007V01 (folderlog becomes independently configurable, superseding ADR003V01's schema driver). Both touch a large, cross-cutting surface of the exact files a Security round would otherwise re-scan -- running another audit round before either lands risks findings against code that is about to be restructured out from under them, and risks the round's own fixes needing to be re-verified again once the registry/folderlog refactors actually happen. Resuming after both land also means the next round gets to audit the refactors themselves, not just what existed before them.
